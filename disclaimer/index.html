  • Doses are reference values only. The App does not calculate doses for a patient. The right dose for a real patient depends on the patient, the procedure, the product and its current labeling, institutional policy and the judgment of a qualified, licensed clinician. Always check a dose against current product labeling and your supervising provider before giving any drug.
